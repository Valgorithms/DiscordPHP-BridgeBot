Outgoing.php : 32

A Discord message on its way out to another network, before anyone has decided what it should look like there.

Text is the raw Discord content, mention markup and all, with the lookup tables needed to render <@1234> as a name. Nothing is escaped or truncated yet, because what has to be escaped and how much will fit are the connector's business: IRC cannot carry a newline, Telegram's HTML mode needs three characters escaped and no more, and the limits are 500 and 4096.

So the relay builds one of these and the connector renders it — using MessageText, which holds the parts that are the same everywhere.
