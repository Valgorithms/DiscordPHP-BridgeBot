Permissions
in package

FinalYes
Permissions.php : 30

Works out which rung of {@see Access} a Discord message's author stands on.

Channel-aware on purpose: getPermissions($channel) resolves the channel's overwrites, so someone denied Manage Messages in one channel is not treated as a moderator there merely because a role grants it server-wide.

public static accessIn(Message $message, Channel|null $channel, string|null $ownerId) : Access|null

For acting on a room from somewhere other than the channel it is bridged to. Rank in one channel says nothing about another: Manage Messages can be granted in a single channel, and a room bridged to #staff must not be reachable by somebody who cannot open #staff.
